MA dissertation · King’s College London · 2023

Understanding Russia’s War in Ukraine: Identity, Threat Perception and Strategic Judgement

George Askaroff

This dissertation examines how Russian identity and perceptions of threat shaped the invasion of Ukraine. Using critical discourse analysis and the concept of ontological security, it explores why Russian leadership came to view a Western-oriented Ukraine as a threat to Russia’s sense of self.
